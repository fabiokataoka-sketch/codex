00 — Principles

Five rules

01

Dark is default

Near-black backgrounds with carbon surfaces. Light theme is opt-in per subtree — the brand lives in the dark.

02

One loud voice

Neon lime is the only saturated color in a view — one accent moment, never large fills of text.

03

Type talks

Archivo 900, uppercase, tight tracking for display. Inter and JetBrains Mono stay quiet.

04

Sharp + pill

Cards and media use 2px radii; buttons and badges are full pills.

05

Motion decides

Fast (150–450ms), one signature ease, everything respects reduced-motion.
