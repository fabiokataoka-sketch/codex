市民税・県民税納税通知書

resident tax bill

City hall is billing your 2025 resident tax: ¥64,500 for the first of four installments. Pay at any konbini before March 15 to avoid late fees.

  1. check the amount

    Page 1, bottom-right box: ¥64,500 for the first installment.

  2. pay at any konbini

    Hand slip ① to the cashier. Cash only at most chains.

  3. keep the stamped receipt

    You may need it for visa renewal or city-hall procedures.

納期限までに納付されない場合は、延滞金が加算されます。

If payment is not made by the due date, a late fee will be added.

口座振替をご希望の方は、同封の申込書をご利用ください。

If you prefer automatic bank transfer, use the enclosed application form.

can i pay this in installments?

Yes — this bill is already split into four installments. The slips are attached in payment order; you only need to pay slip ① by March 15.

what if i already moved to another city?

You still owe this bill: resident tax is charged by the city you lived in on January 1. Your new city will bill you next year.
